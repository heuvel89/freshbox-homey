Your HelloFresh box in Homey.

See when your next box arrives and in which time slot, which recipes are in it and whether a week has been skipped. Use it in Flows: open the roller shutter on delivery day, but only when the week is not skipped; get a reminder the evening before; or a notification when the time slot starts or the menu choice closes. Nine colourful dashboard widgets show this week, next week, live delivery status, a countdown, the menu deadline, a "box is outside" warning, the pantry items you need yourself, upcoming weeks and a compact status bar.

Works together with the Bring! Shopping List app: the items you need from your own pantry (olive oil, butter…) can go straight onto your Bring! list, automatically or with one tap.

Log in with the e-mail address and password of your HelloFresh account, or with a login link from your e-mail. Works for HelloFresh Netherlands and Belgium.

This app is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with HelloFresh. It uses the same connection as the HelloFresh website, which is not an official API and may change without notice.
